# Governance Specification

## 1. Purpose

The OSEF Governance Specification defines how decisions are proposed, reviewed, approved, implemented, recorded, and evolved throughout the lifecycle of the Operating Systems Engineering Framework and every project built with it.

Governance exists to preserve architectural integrity, engineering quality, knowledge continuity, traceability, and long-term sustainability.

Its purpose is not to slow development.

Its purpose is to ensure that evolution remains disciplined, deliberate, and accountable.

---

## 2. Scope

This specification applies to:

- OSEF normative documents;
- OSEF reference implementations;
- engineering decisions;
- architectural changes;
- runtime changes;
- quality and security decisions;
- knowledge assets;
- lifecycle updates;
- versioning and release activities.

Any significant change to OSEF or to an OSEF-based project should follow the governance rules defined in this specification.

---

## 3. Governance Principles

Every governance decision should preserve:

- Engineering integrity
- Architectural consistency
- Specification compliance
- Human accountability
- Knowledge preservation
- Traceability
- Continuous improvement

Governance must remain aligned with the OSEF Manifesto and the Core Principles.
